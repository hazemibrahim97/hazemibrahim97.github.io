Daily reading

Daily habit

  • Techmeme daily Aggregated tech news on one page
  • Nature Briefing daily Science news, research policy, publishing
  • Axios AI+ daily AI business and policy scoops
  • Money Stuff (Matt Levine) daily Finance, markets, prediction markets (free by email; the web version is paywalled)

Platforms, algorithms and tech policy

  • Tech Policy Press daily Platform governance and regulation, often written by researchers
  • AI as Normal Technology weekly Narayanan and Kapoor on AI claims and evaluation (formerly AI Snake Oil)
  • Understanding AI weekly Timothy B. Lee's explainers on AI and its policy stakes
  • Platformer weekly Casey Newton on trust and safety and platforms (partly paywalled)
  • Garbage Day weekly Internet culture and how content spreads

Politics and computational social science

  • Good Authority daily Political scientists on current events
  • Conspicuous Cognition weekly Dan Williams on misinformation and polarization research
  • Silver Bulletin weekly Polling, forecasting, prediction markets (partly paywalled)
  • Strength In Numbers weekly G. Elliott Morris on polling and public opinion data

Science of science and scholarly publishing

  • Retraction Watch daily Research integrity, retractions, citation manipulation
  • Retraction Watch: Weekend Reads weekly Saturday roundup
  • The Scholarly Kitchen daily Publishing industry: open access, paywalls, metrics
  • Experimental History weekly Adam Mastroianni's metascience essays

Tech, AI and the economy

  • Noahpinion weekly Noah Smith on the AI economy, industrial policy, geopolitics
  • Exponential View weekly Azeem Azhar on AI, energy and the economy
  • Stratechery weekly Ben Thompson on tech strategy (one free article a week)
